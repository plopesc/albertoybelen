# Arte del mapa

`world-16bit.webp` es un fondo original generado con la herramienta integrada
`image_gen`, para esta tarea, siguiendo la preferencia de una estética de 16 bits
inspirada en Super Nintendo. No usa fotografías personales, sprites de terceros,
personajes ni mapas de juegos existentes. No es una captura de un juego comercial.

El PNG generado se convirtió a WebP (calidad 88) con ImageMagick; el recurso se
sirve localmente desde el repositorio, sin servicios externos en tiempo de ejecución.
El camino, los hitos, sus estados y el indicador se dibujan por separado en SVG.

## Prompt utilizado

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original environment background for a playable vertical scrolling web game, portrait aspect ratio 3:8, ideally 768x2048.
Primary request: a richly illustrated 16-bit console era overworld landscape, with the craft and detail of a beautiful Super Nintendo game background, original artwork. This must feel distinctly 16-bit: small crisp pixels, sophisticated clustered pixel shading, many harmonious colors, lush organic silhouettes, atmospheric depth and lovingly painted scenery, NOT large flat rectangles or 8-bit block graphics.
Composition: one continuous tall map seen from a high overhead three-quarter game camera, covering the whole frame. From bottom to top: lush emerald forest occupying bottom 30%; turquoise river with rocky banks and a little waterfall crossing horizontally at 35-43% from the bottom; sunny terracotta canyon occupying 48-73% from bottom; blue alpine mountains and snowy summit in top 25%. Natural transitions between biomes, layered ridges, small rock faces, tree canopies, little wildflowers, sunlit grass, sparkling water and distant clouds. Keep the central 60% of the width relatively open and traversable, with most tall decorative trees and rock formations framing the left and right edges, because a separately drawn winding trail and game markers will be overlaid there. Add a small canvas adventurer tent in a forest clearing off-center, no people.
Lighting: golden daylight, luminous highlights, deep cool shadows, joyful adventurous mood. Palette: emerald, teal, warm ochre and terracotta, blue-violet rock shadows, cream snow. Organic contours, fine 1-3 pixel details and subtle dithering, sharp pixel art, no blur.
Constraints: BACKGROUND ENVIRONMENT ONLY. No roads, trails, tracks, bridges, characters, numbers, text, signs, labels, checkpoints, finish arch, presents, HUD, border, logo or watermark. Do not reproduce any Nintendo characters or existing game map. No isometric floating islands. Fill the portrait composition edge to edge.
```
